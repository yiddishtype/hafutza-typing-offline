**Hafutza Typing for Windows** - types up Yiddish (and Hebrew, English, Spanish, French) videos and recordings on your own computer, with no internet, and translates them to Yiddish, English, Hebrew, Spanish and French.

Download **HafutzaTyping-Setup.exe** below and open it. (Have version 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.3.1, 1.4 or 1.5? Just press the program's **Update** button. Have version 1.0? Install this one over it. Your typed texts and the downloaded parts are kept.)

New in 1.6:
- **The AI answers every message** you write in the chat and starts on it right away, with no internet.
- **Chat in Hebrew**: write to the AI in Hebrew and it answers in Hebrew, shown right to left. Hebrew requests change the text or the program just like Yiddish or English ones.
- **A link for any AI**: when the program's AI can't do something, it offers **Copy the link for any AI**. Paste it into any AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...). It holds your request and a link to the program's full code and a guide for that AI.
- **Install an update another AI made**: a new box in the **Update** window. Paste the GitHub link that AI gives you. The program checks that it really is a copy of Hafutza Typing and that the file is in it, warns you that we did not make or check it, and installs it when you say yes. Your typed texts and downloaded parts are kept.

New in 1.5:
- **The AI ball**: a round **AI** ball on the side of the window. Click it and the AI opens as a **chat on the side** (about 4 inches wide); click it again to close it.
- **Chat with the AI like texting**: write in the box at the bottom and press Enter. Ask for a change (fix a word, shorten a line, translate, rewrite a part, in Yiddish or English) and it **makes the change in the text right away** and says what it changed. Every change has an **Undo** button. You can also just ask it a question, and it answers in the chat.
- **Faster AI**: it answers much quicker, also while a video is being typed (the typing waits a moment for it).
- **One download**: after installing, the program downloads everything once, **the AI included**, with one progress bar (about 6.6 GB). Then it all works with no internet.
- **The three-dots menu (⋮)** at the top: **Settings** (brightness, sound, a choice of 21 fonts for the typed words, colours, sizes and places), **AI in the full window**, **My add-ons**, **Check for update** and **Reset to normal**. Everything you set is remembered.
- **Tell the AI how the program should look**, like *make the buttons blue and bigger and move the AI button to the left*. It changes it right away, with no internet, and every change has **Undo**.
- **Add-ons**: ask the AI for a new button, like *a button that copies all the text*, and it builds it. Turn add-ons on or off, or delete them, under **My add-ons**.
- **Send to Avi**: when a request is too big for the AI, it offers a **Send to Avi** button. The request goes to us (with no internet it waits and goes by itself later).
- **Ask a different AI** (this one needs the internet): put in your own Gemini, OpenAI or Claude key (kept only on your computer), or copy a ready-made request to a free AI website and paste its answer back. The program checks the answer and applies it with **Undo**.

New in 1.4:
- **AI: fix words** (new button at the top): write what to fix, in Yiddish or English, for example *change X to Y* or *write Reb, not Rabbi*. The AI shows the word changes it suggests; untick any you don't want and press **OK**. Nothing changes without OK.
- It works **while a video is still being typed or translated**: the lines still to come get the fix too. The .txt and .srt files and the Watch-and-fix view get the fix as well. Tick **Also remember these** to have it fixed in the next videos too (Learned words).
- It runs on your own computer, with no internet. The AI is a **separate one-time download (2.7 GB)**: the program offers it the first time you press the button. It needs about **8 GB of memory** in the computer and usually answers in 10 to 40 seconds. The rest of the program works just the same without it.

New in 1.3.1:
- Facebook video links now come with the picture, not only the sound.

New in 1.3:
- The **Update** button now opens a window with two parts: **1. Update - what's new** shows what the newest version adds, with one button to update; **2. Request an update**: write what you want changed or added (in Yiddish or English, with the on-screen Yiddish keyboard if you like) and press **Send**. It reaches us, and the program says "Got it, your request was sent."
- No internet when you press Send? The request is kept on your computer and goes by itself the next time the program is open with the internet on.
- The **Update** button at the top turns **blue** when a new version is ready (it stays plain when you have the newest).

New in 1.2:
- **Watch and fix**: after a video is typed, press **Watch the video with the text on it, and fix the text**. The video plays with the typing and the translation on it, line by line.
- **Play / Pause** (or the space bar), and buttons to go **back or forward 5, 10 or 15 seconds**. Click any line in the list to jump there.
- Fix a line: erase it, type it again, or use the on-screen **Yiddish keyboard**. Press **Save the fix** (or Enter): the .txt and .srt files are written again with the fix.
- **The program learns your fixes**: a word you fix is remembered and fixed by itself in the next videos (in the typing and in the translations). See or delete what it learned under **Learned words**. (The big speech and translation models are not retrained; it is your own word list.)
- **My typed videos**: open any video typed with 1.2 again to watch and fix. (Videos typed before 1.2 have no line timings: type them again to watch them.)
- **Video links now come with the picture** (up to 720p), so a pasted link plays in the player like a dropped file. If a site gives only the sound, the text shows on a dark screen.
- Each typed text is also saved as subtitles (.srt) next to the .txt.

New in 1.1:
- **Update** button (top right): checks for a new version and installs it with one click (downloads only the program, not the big speech parts). It also quietly checks when the program opens.
- Big **drop box**: drop one or more video/audio files on it (or click it to choose them); several files are typed one after another.
- **Paste a video link** (YouTube, Facebook, other sites, or a link to an .mp4/.mp3): the program gets the video (this step needs the internet), then types it as usual. Private or sign-in-only videos can't be fetched: save them and drop the file in.
- Right-click a video or sound file in Windows: **Open with > Hafutza Typing**.

- If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. (The program is new and not signed by a paid certificate, so Windows does not know it yet.)
- The first time it opens it downloads the translation, the Hebrew / English / Spanish / French parts and the AI once (about 6.6 GB). After that it works with no internet.

Needs: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), about 5 GB of free disk space, 8 GB of memory (4 GB works but slowly).

Files: HafutzaTyping-Setup.exe = the full installer. HafutzaTyping-Update.exe and update.json = used by the Update button.

Models: ivrit-ai Yiddish and Hebrew Whisper turbo (Apache-2.0), OpenAI Whisper large-v3-turbo (MIT), Meta NLLB-200 distilled 1.3B (CC-BY-NC-4.0, non-commercial use). Speech is read by faster-whisper / CTranslate2 (MIT). Video links: yt-dlp (Unlicense).
